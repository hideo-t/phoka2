FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

QCan it be used as a licensed trucking office?＋
The requirements differ with the site conditions and intended use. We will show you how it has been approached so far and discuss and propose a specification and layout with the license application in mind. Please contact us for details.
QWhat about fixed asset tax and building confirmation?＋
Because a trailer home is classified as a vehicle, it needs no building confirmation as a building would, and no real estate acquisition tax or fixed asset tax applies. Whether this applies is judged by the state of installation, so we will confirm and advise on the details.
QHow much land does installation need?＋
It depends on the size and number of units and the local terrain. We will discuss it individually, including the arrangement relative to your depot.
QWhat if we want to move base after installation?＋
You can transport and redeploy it without demolition. We can also discuss moving or adding units as your business expands, contracts or relocates.
